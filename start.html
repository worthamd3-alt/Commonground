Setting up

  1. Open the link in Safari Not Chrome, and not from inside another app. Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen This is the step people skip. Notifications only reach you from the home screen version — a browser tab will never buzz.
  3. Open it from your home screen and sign up Use your work email. Say yes when it asks about notifications.

Worth trying

  • Add a photo and fill in your profile
  • Post something to the feed
  • Join your department
  • Answer the question on the home screen
  • Pick a side in the blood drive
Never post patient information.

No names, no room numbers, nothing that could identify someone. This app is for the people who work here, not the people we treat.
